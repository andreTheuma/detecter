- D-06: Generated monitors must declare their dependency on `agm_engine` through explicit remote calls.
- D-07: Plan 04-03 generated-monitor characterization tests are a locked behavioral gate and must pass without semantic weakening.
- D-08: Every supplied thesis chapter receives an evidence-backed `amended` or `verified unchanged` disposition after refactoring.
- D-09: Phase artifacts and source changes remain uncommitted until the user reviews them.
