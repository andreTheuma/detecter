## Required Distinctions

- **Generated code vs reusable runtime:** transition data, reduction closures, receive/ETS boundaries, and continuation calls are generated; inference and aggregation are reusable `agm_engine` functions.
- **Synthesis time vs runtime:** `maxhml_agm_codegen` constructs Erlang AST at synthesis time; `agm_engine` evaluates supplied transition data and reductions at monitor runtime.
- **Exact event vs monitoring consequence:** exact literal recovery is metadata; proceeding depends on one complete consequence signature.
- **Withholding vs verdict:** every ambiguity or unproven case returns `{withhold, Reason}` and emits neither `yes` nor `no`.
- **Focused checks vs complete build:** EUnit validates source/runtime behavior; `latexmk` independently validates the complete thesis build.
